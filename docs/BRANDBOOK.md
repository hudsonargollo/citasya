# CitasYa Brandbook & Visual Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity & Philosophy

### Name
**CitasYa** (*"Appointments Now"* in Spanish)

### Mission
To empower independent professionals, service businesses, salons, spas, clinics, wellness centers, and consultants with an instantaneous, frictionless appointment scheduling and multi-vendor marketplace platform.

### Core Values
- **Speed & Agility**: Booking an appointment should take under 30 seconds.
- **Trust & Reliability**: Secure scheduling, verified providers, and transparent pricing.
- **Universal Versatility**: Designed not just for beauty salons, but for any service-based professional (healthcare, wellness, fitness, coaching, legal, pet care).

## 3. Typography

- **Headings (Display / H1 - H6)**: **Poppins** (Weights: 600 SemiBold, 700 Bold) — Modern, geometric, clean, and approachable.
- **Body & UI Text**: **Open Sans** (Weights: 300 Light, 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 600 SemiBold) — Highly legible at small sizes across mobile and web.

## 4. Logo Proposals

### Concept A: The "Instant Clock & Checkmark" (Primary)
- **Symbol**: A modern minimalist clock face merging seamlessly with a dynamic checkmark arrow.
- **Meaning**: Represents real-time booking confirmation and punctuality.
- **Wordmark**: "Citas**Ya**" in bold Poppins (Primary Blue for "Citas", Emerald for "Ya").

### Concept B: The Interlocking Calendar Grid
- **Symbol**: Four rounded calendar appointment cards intersecting in a subtle 3D isometric perspective.
- **Meaning**: Multi-vendor marketplace, organization, and harmony.
