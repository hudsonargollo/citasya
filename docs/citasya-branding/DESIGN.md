## Brand & Style

This design system channels the warmth, lush greenery, and entrepreneurial drive of Santa Cruz de la Sierra into a high-converting, joyful service booking ecosystem. Inspired by modern Southeast Asian super-apps like Janjiyuk, the aesthetic merges approachable tech with high-efficiency booking flows.

### Brand Personality & Tone
- **Warm & Optimistic:** A breezy, welcoming tropical atmosphere rooted in Santa Cruz identity ("Verde Cruceño"), avoiding sterile clinical tropes while preserving clinical and professional trust.
- **Fast & Direct:** Optimized for frictionless 3-step appointment confirmations via WhatsApp and QR-ready web flows.
- **Vibrant Neo-Modernism:** Elevated card structures, crisp micro-borders, vibrant status chips, playful stat badges, and micro-interactions that make schedule coordination feel celebratory rather than administrative.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
- **Urban Professionals & Everyday Cambas:** Busy clients booking healthcare, wellness, salons, legal, and automotive appointments on mobile devices.
- **Local Service Providers:** Independent specialists and modern clinics seeking an organized, high-end digital storefront.
- **Emotional Response:** Instant relief, absolute clarity on timing/pricing, and delight in intuitive booking.

## Colors

The color palette reflects the lush foliage of the Santa Cruz department anchored by authoritative night slates and high-urgency accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary — Verde Cruceño (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** Represents growth, health, and local Santa Cruz vitality. Used for prime booking buttons, confirmation states, active calendar date selections, and brand logos.
- **Secondary — Modern Cyan (`#06B6D4`) & Tropical Mint (`#34D399`):** Represents digital speed and tropical clarity. Used for interactive service categories, time-slot selection chips, and data metrics.
- **Tertiary — Camba Amber (`#F59E0B`):** High-energy attention grabber reserved for high-priority CTAs, real-time availability counters ("Only 1 slot left"), and flash-booking prompts.
- **Night Slate Base (`#0F172A`):** Deep ink slate for razor-sharp typography, key icons, and anchored navigation headers.
- **Surface Canvas:** Soft Snow (`#F8FAFC`) with contextual highlights bathed in Pale Mint tint (`#F0FDF4`).

### Functional Tokens
- **Surface Container Low:** `#FFFFFF` (pure white for cards to pop off the soft snow background).
- **Surface Container High:** `#F1F5F9` (for secondary disabled blocks and input containers).
- **Border Subtle:** `#E2E8F0` (light crisp outline to maintain clean neo-modern separation).
- **Destructive/Alert:** `#EF4444` (cancellations and errors).

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is used globally across display, body, and UI labels. Its geometric clarity, wide proportions, and rounded letterforms evoke modernity and warmth without compromising high-density calendar and pricing scannability.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) with extra bold weights (`700` and `800`) to anchor hero search forms, specialty titles, and promotional banners.
- **Numbers & Rates:** Financial rates (in Bolivianos `Bs.`) and calendar time indicators use `font-weight: 700` with tabular lining figures to maintain scan efficiency across schedule grids.
- **Micro-Copy & Badges:** `label-sm` and `label-md` feature uppercase tracking (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) for status markers ("DISPONIBLE HOY", "VERIFICADO", "SANTA CRUZ").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a mobile-first philosophy accommodating quick booking on Android and iOS devices, then adapting gracefully to full web desktop booking dashboards.

### Responsive Breakpoint Framework
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Sticky bottom-anchored booking sheets and horizontal scrolling chip carousels for dates and specialties.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin-md: 1.5rem`. Split views pairing the professional summary with live schedule calendars.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxed at `1280px` centered with `margin-lg: 2.5rem` and `gutter-lg: 1.5rem`. Sticky checkout/booking rails alongside comprehensive doctor/salon portfolios.

### Spacing Grid
Spacing strictly adheres to an 8pt system (with 4pt sub-steps via `space-xs`), keeping vertical card rhythm compact and clean.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **ambient, soft tinted shadows** combined with **subtle low-contrast borders** to produce clean, modern floating surfaces with a tropical touch.

### Depth Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base page canvas (`#F8FAFC`). No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Background `#FFFFFF`, Border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, Shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`. Used for provider cards, service listings, and search bars.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Active Floating Cards):** Border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, Shadow: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. The primary emerald tint adds brand life and lift.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Time-Slot Pickers & Floating Action Buttons):** Shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.14), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.08)`.
- **Level 4 (Sticky Booking Bar):** Border top `1px solid #E2E8F0`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and upward shadow `0 -4px 16px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design uses **Rounded (level 2)** shaping across containers and inputs, infused with signature **full pill capsules** for interactives and status chips to maintain the Janjiyuk-inspired lively consumer vibe.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Cards, Modals & Containers (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) gives specialist profiles and service cards a friendly, contemporary framing.
- **Inputs & Secondary Containers (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) for form inputs, calendar tiles, and review containers.
- **Pill Elements (`rounded-full`):** `9999px` strictly enforced on interactive badges, category pills, action buttons, time-slot selection chips, and user avatar wrappers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#059669`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height 48px on mobile / 52px on desktop. On hover, shifts to `#047857` with slight upward translation (`-1px`).
- **Urgent Instant CTA:** Background `#F59E0B`, text `#0F172A`, font `label-lg`, pill-shaped with subtle pulse micro-animation. Used for "Reservar Ahora" or "Cupo Inmediato".
- **Secondary / Ghost:** White background, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Hover border shifts to `#059669` with mint wash `#F0FDF4`.

### Neo-Modern Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, `1.5rem` border-radius, and a `1px solid #F1F5F9` perimeter.
- Feature an inner padding of `space-md` to `space-lg`.
- Specialist card headers nest a verified badge (`#059669` with a white check) overlapping the provider's rounded profile picture.

### Interactive Time & Date Chips
- **Resting:** Pill-shaped, background `#F8FAFC`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`, typography `label-md`.
- **Selected:** Background `#059669`, border `1px solid #059669`, text `#FFFFFF`, with micro-drop shadow.
- **Unavailable/Booked:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#94A3B8`, strikethrough styling, unclickable.

### Playful Badges & Statistic Counters
- **Highlight Pill Badges:** Pale background (`#ECFDF5`), emerald text (`#047857`), rounded-full, with an icon (e.g., "⚡ Confirmación WhatsApp en 2 min").
- **Stat Badges:** Compact rounded boxes inside provider listings displaying rating score (`★ 4.9`), completed bookings (`+500 citas`), and neighborhood indicator (`Equipetrol, Santa Cruz`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Floating white or `#F8FAFC` background with `1rem` radius.
- Inactive border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`; focused border `1.5px solid #059669` accompanied by a `3px` focus ring in `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.15)`.
- Integrated leading icons (location pin, calendar, magnifying glass) in `#06B6D4` or `#059669`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Radios feature a dual-ring emerald indicator (`#059669`).
- Checkboxes use `rounded-md` (6px) with an emerald fill and a bold white check icon on active states.
